# Contributing

We welcome corrections, clarity edits, and worked examples. Use synthetic data or an example you have permission to publish. State the original claim, what each rival predicts, and what evidence changed the verdict.

Keep contributions scoped to the public protocol. Remove identifiers, internal links, operational details, and any data you are not authorized to share. Do not use public issues or pull requests to report sensitive incidents; see [Security and Disclosure](SECURITY.md).

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you agree to license it under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE) and confirm that you have the rights necessary to do so.
